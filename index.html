Product Philosophy: How I Approach Problems & Trade-Offs

Start with firsthand user friction

Every solution begins by observing real struggle—classmates anxious over placement rounds, or clients with unarticulated operational needs.

Ruthless MVP prioritization

Under tight constraints, deliberately cut non-essential features (e.g., deferring proctoring in Smart Hire AI) to ship a reliable core value proposition fast.

Listen, iterate, and surface latent needs

Putting a working v1 in front of customers uncovers critical requirements that the initial brief never surfaced, such as accountability ledgers.
